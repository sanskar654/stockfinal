1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is an institutional-grade, end-to-end Machine Learning (ML) Trading 
and Automated Risk Management Platform designed for Indian equities (NIFTY 50).

In conventional retail trading, over 90% of intraday traders lose money due to:
  - Emotional decision-making (FOMO, panic selling).
  - Lack of systematic risk management (not setting or respecting stop losses).
  - Information overload without clear directional probability.

Our Solution:
This platform acts as an intelligent "AI Trading Co-Pilot". It continuously 
streams real-time tick data from the stock exchange via FYERS API, processes 
40+ technical indicators, executes dual XGBoost Machine Learning models, and 
provides traders with clear, mathematical, and actionable guidance:
  - Exact Entry Price Zones
  - Dynamic ATR-based Stop Losses (Capital Protection)
  - 30-Minute Tactical & Full-Day EOD Targets
  - Quantity-scaled Profit/Loss Breakdowns in exact Rupees (INR)
  - 24/7 Automated Trade Monitoring via WebSockets with Trailing Stop-Losses

3. DETAILED TAB-BY-TAB WALKTHROUGH (WHAT EACH SCREEN DOES)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAB 1: 📊 DASHBOARD (Market Surveillance & NIFTY 50 Universe)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Gives the trader an instant bird's-eye view of market sentiment.
• Key Features:
  - Live NIFTY 50 Ticker Grid: Real-time prices, percentage moves, and sectors.
  - Top Opportunities Scanner: Automatically highlights stocks showing strong 
    bullish or bearish ML signals.
  - Market Stress Detector: Monitors overall index drop. If the broader market 
    falls sharply, defensive alerts protect the user from aggressive buying.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAB 2: 🎯 TRADE DISCOVERY (Smart Order & Strategy Configurator)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Allows the trader to configure order parameters before entering a trade.
• Key Features:
  - Quantity & Limit Price Input: Select number of shares (e.g., 10, 50, 100).
  - Pre-Trade Validation: Evaluates whether the proposed entry price is inside the 
    optimal statistical entry zone or too close to the Stop Loss.
  - Margin & Capital Estimator: Instantly calculates the total investment required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAB 3: 🛡️ RISK MANAGEMENT & CO-PILOT (The Core Value Engine)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Eliminates guesswork by displaying exact numeric exit levels and 
  plain-English execution guidance.
• Key Features:
  1. Numbers-First Hero Section:
     - Live Market Price (LTP) with real-time updates.
     - Dynamic Stop Loss: Calculated as [Entry ± (1.5 × ATR-14)] to stay outside 
       normal market noise.
     - 30-Minute Intraday Target: Fast tactical profit target.
     - Full-Day EOD Target: Session swing target.
     - Suggested Entry Zone & Risk/Reward Ratio.
  2. Visual Price Ladder:
     - Graphical track illustrating exact relative distances:
       Stop Loss ──► Entry Zone ──► Live Price ──► 30m Target ──► EOD Target.
  3. AI Action Playbook:
     - 🟢 BUY SIGNAL: When ML forecast is positive and confidence is high.
     - 🔴 BEARISH / SELL: When ML forecast is negative.
     - 🟡 WAIT FOR PULLBACK: When RSI is overbought (>78) or price is extended.
     - 3-step structured guidance: Entry Plan, Stop Loss Rule, and Profit Strategy.
  4. Quantity-Scaled P/L Table:
     - Multiplies every metric by the selected quantity (e.g., 10 shares):
       * Capital Required: 10 × ₹3,190 = ₹31,900
       * Max Loss if Stop Loss hit: -₹450 (-1.41%)
       * Potential Profit if Target hit: +₹900 (+2.82%)
  5. Interactive What-If Scenario Simulator:
     - Drag-and-drop price slider (-8% to +8%) that recalculates exact Rupee P/L 
       and signals when Stop Loss or Target is hit.
  6. One-Click Order Execution:
     - Records the trade into the database and registers it with the live supervisor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAB 4: 📈 TECHNICAL ANALYTICS (Multi-Indicator Confluence & Charts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Provides deep-dive technical verification for analytical traders.
• Key Features:
  - Interactive Candlestick Chart: Switch smoothly between 15-Minute (28 bars) 
    and 30-Minute (14 aggregated bars) timeframes.
  - Core Technical Gauges: RSI-14 (Overbought/Oversold), MACD Signal crossover, 
    20-Day Volatility %, and 20-Period Volume Ratio.
  - Trade Score (0-100): Combines indicator confluence and ML probability.
  - Direct Cross-Link to Risk Management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAB 5: 👤 PROFILE & LIVE TRADE LEDGER (Performance & Position Tracking)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Purpose: Tracks executed trades, verified win rates, and live active positions.
• Key Features:
  - Real Performance Metrics: Win Rate %, Net P/L, Profit Factor, and Total Trades 
    computed from actual database ledger records.
  - Active Trade Monitor: Live tracking of open positions with current unrealized 
    profit/loss and trailing stop-loss status.

4. INTELLIGENT REAL-TIME ALERTS & CO-PILOT (WebSocket Feed)
================================================================================
The platform features an active background monitoring engine:
• Continuous Monitoring: Evaluates active trades every 60 seconds against real-time prices.
• Dynamic Trailing Stop-Loss: As a trade moves in profit, the Stop-Loss automatically 
  trails upward to lock in profits.
• Trend Reversal & Crash Guard: If the ML model detects an unexpected market reversal, 
  it pushes an instant alert over WebSockets (/ws/alerts) advising the user to 
  exit immediately and protect capital.

5. MACHINE LEARNING ARCHITECTURE & ACCURACY METRICS
================================================================================
• Framework: XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting)
• Why XGBoost? Gold-standard for tabular financial time-series, built-in L1/L2 
  regularization to prevent overfitting, and ultra-fast sub-15ms inference.

• Models in Production:
  1. Direction Classifier (XGBClassifier):
     - Predicts probability of price moving UP vs. DOWN over the next 30 minutes.
     - Directional Accuracy: ~68.6% - 71.4% (Validated via 5-Fold TimeSeries Cross-Validation).
  2. Return Regressor (XGBRegressor):
     - Predicts expected percentage return.
     - Mean Absolute Error (MAE): ~0.35% - 0.38%.

• Training & Retraining:
  - Features: 40+ scale-invariant technical indicators.
  - Automated Retraining Pipeline: Scheduled at 6:00 PM IST every weekday 
    (after Indian market close) to adapt to new market regimes without drift.
